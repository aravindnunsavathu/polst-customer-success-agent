# **2. Numbers owned**

The single most important section. "Own" means: it appears on the VP CS scorecard, CS is accountable for it, and CS has the authority to act on it.

| **Metric** | **Definition** | **Proposed ownership** | **Agreed** | **Target FY[XX]** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Net Revenue Retention (NRR)** | Revenue from prior-period cohort in current period ÷ revenue from that cohort in prior period. Consumption-based. | **Own** |  | 120% |
| **Gross Revenue Retention (GRR)** | Same, excluding all expansion | **Own** |  | 90% |
| **Logo retention** | % accounts still generating ≥1 campaign/quarter | **Own** (secondary — see note) |  | 90% |
| **Campaign volume growth per account** | 90-day rolling volume vs. trailing baseline | **Own** |  | 120% |
| **Net new departments live** | Departments running ≥1 campaign, net of departments gone dark | **Own or Influence** — decide |  | 1/qtr |
| **New logo ARR** |  | **Not owned** — Sales | Sales | — |
| **Time to first value** | Days from contract to first customer-confirmed result | **Own** |  | 15 days |
| **Committed volume conversion** | % of revenue under commitment vs. ad-hoc | **Own or Influence** — decide (depends on §5) |  | 10% |
| **Support CSAT / ticket resolution** [Link on CSAT](https://www.reddit.com/r/customerexperience/comments/yk6mak/how_to_measure_customer_experience_satisfaction/) |  | **Own** |  | 90% |

**Note on logo retention:** under per-campaign pricing, logo retention will systematically overstate health. An account can lose 80% of its value and still count as retained. It is a useful hygiene metric but must never be the headline. NRR is the headline.

# **5. Commercial model questions to resolve**

Consumption pricing raises questions that are not CS decisions but that CS cannot operate without answers to.

| **Question** | **Why it matters to CS** | **Answer** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Is there any committed / minimum volume today, or is it purely pay-as-you-go? | Determines whether there is a renewal moment at all, and whether forecasting is possible | No |
| Do unused campaigns expire, roll over, or refund? | Drives the utilization play and end-of-term behavior | Expire |
| Is there volume-tier pricing? At what break points? | These break points *are* your expansion milestones | Yes |
| What is the contract term and notice period? | Sets the intervention clock | Monthly |
| Can a department be added mid-term without a new contract? | Determines whether CS and Sales collaboration | No |
| What does a campaign cost us to deliver? | Tells you whether volume growth is actually margin-enhancing | NA |
| Is there a floor below which an account is unprofitable to serve? | Feeds the coverage model | NA |

# **7. Explicit non-goals and guardrails**

*Naming these prevents the function drifting into them.*

- **CS is not a volume-pushing function.** Because Polst prices per campaign, there is a standing temptation to drive campaign count for its own sake. Campaigns that produce no customer result do not repeat, so this borrows from next quarter. Every volume metric is reviewed alongside a quality metric.

- **CS is not a substitute for product-led onboarding.** Where a CSM is compensating for a product gap, that gap gets logged and routed, not absorbed silently.

- **CS does not own new logo acquisition.**
