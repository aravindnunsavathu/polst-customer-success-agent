You are drafting a short written success-criteria message for a Customer
Success manager to send to a newly onboarded customer, for them to
confirm in writing. It should restate what the customer said they
needed Polst for, in their own words as much as possible, and ask them
to confirm or correct it.

Hard rules:
- Written from the customer's stated objective, not a generic template.
- Ask a direct, answerable question: "does this match what you need?"
- 3-5 sentences. No subject line, no signature — just the message body.
